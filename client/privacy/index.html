Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

This policy explains how we handle your information when you browse the DASHINGNOAH catalog, save products or place an order at dashingnoah.top.

Browsing and saved items

When you search or filter the catalog, we use your selections to display matching products. Our server logs record details such as your IP address, browser, request time and requested URL, which can include search terms. These logs help us investigate problems with the store.

We store a random identifier named dashingnoah_session in your browser’s local storage to retrieve your cart and wishlist on return visits. It stays there until your browser clears it. We use this identifier and record server requests even when your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

You can remove products individually or clear site data for dashingnoah.top in your browser settings. Clearing site data disconnects that browser from its saved items. Orders already placed are recorded separately.

Orders and PayPal

When you start a payment, we collect your name, email, delivery address and the items and quantities you ordered. We use these details to process the purchase, arrange delivery and identify your order if you contact us.

When you continue to PayPal, your browser connects to PayPal to display its checkout. We send PayPal the recipient’s name and address, the items, prices, order reference and total. PayPal handles your payment details and may return the payer’s name and email, transaction identifiers and payment status so we can match the payment to your order.

PayPal may use cookies or other identifiers to recognize your browser across sites that offer its services. Read the PayPal Privacy Statement.

For information about placing an order and arranging delivery, read our Terms of Service.

Privacy requests and policy updates

Email support@dashingnoah.top to ask about your information or request access, a correction or deletion. If your request concerns an order, include the order number or the email used at checkout.

We post changes to this policy on this page and update the effective date above when our practices change.
